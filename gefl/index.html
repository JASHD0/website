Rules

How this thing runs

📋 Format

  • 10 teams, no divisions — seeded on overall record.
  • 14-week regular season, 6-team playoff (weeks 15–17).
  • Seeds 1 & 2 get a first-round bye.

🔀 Draft

  • 2026 order drawn randomly.
  • From 2027 on, final standings set the next year's order.

💰 Waivers

FAAB — everyone bids their own budget on the wire, no priority list.

🔥 Rivalry Weeks

Weeks 10–14 replay your top grudges from the survey, with home and away flipped. Thanksgiving (Week 12) is locked as the marquee rivalry week.
